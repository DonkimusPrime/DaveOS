# CLAUDE.md — People

One file per person, named `firstname-lastname.md`, created from `_TEMPLATE-Person.md`.
People are cross-cutting: one file each even when they span two companies, linked from both.

## Rules
- Write what was observed, not what it says about them. "Went quiet after the pricing question,
  followed up in writing two days later" — not "conflict-avoidant". Behaviour, context, and date;
  the reader draws their own conclusion.
- This applies hardest to the difficult material. Anything about performance, motivation, or a
  strained dynamic gets written the same way: specific, dated, and anchored to something that
  actually happened. No traits, no labels, no scoring.
- Record only what the owner has said. Never infer a reporting line, a motivation, or a concern
  from a job title, a company chart, or another person's file.
- These are notes for managing a relationship well — not a dossier and not a performance record.
  If a line would be damaging read out of context, that's a reason to write it more precisely, or
  to leave it out and raise it with the owner directly.
- After a 1:1: newest entry at the top of "Recent 1:1s", and anything agreed moves into open items
  with an owner.
