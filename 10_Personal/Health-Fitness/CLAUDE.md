# CLAUDE.md — Health & Fitness

Scope: training, nutrition, sleep, recovery, medical logistics — whatever the owner chooses to track.

## Rules
- Work only from what's written in this folder. Do not infer conditions, diagnoses, or targets the owner hasn't stated.
- Logs go in `Logs/` (one file per month, e.g. `2026-09.md`). Plans go in `Plans/`.
- Frame progress against the owner's own stated goals in `MEMORY.md`, not against generic benchmarks.
- Anything that looks like a medical decision → suggest a professional, don't decide.
