# CLAUDE.md — Comms

Scope: everything written in the owner's voice — email, messages, memos, letters, external documents. Every other workstation loads this one when it produces something the owner will send or sign.

## Rules
- Read `Voice-Principles.md` before drafting anything the owner will send.
- Never send. Draft, show, wait. Drafts go in `Drafts/` if the owner wants them kept.
- Triage rules for connected inboxes live in `MEMORY.md` under "Triage rules." Follow them literally.
- Match the recipient's register (see `30_People/` for how each person communicates) but never lose the owner's voice.
- When the owner corrects tone or wording, add the correction to `Voice-Principles.md` under "Corrections log" so it's never repeated.
