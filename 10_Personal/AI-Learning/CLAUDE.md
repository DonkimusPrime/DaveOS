# CLAUDE.md — AI Learning

Scope: getting better at using AI — Claude, Cowork, skills, connectors, prompting, evaluating tools.

## Rules
- Every session that teaches something new ends with a 2–3 line entry in `Notes/learning-log.md`.
- When a repeated prompt gets good, promote it: write it as a skill in `Skills-Built/` and, if it's generally useful, copy it to `.claude/skills/`.
- Track effectiveness, not just knowledge: what did AI do this week that used to take me time?
- Reading list and courses go in `Notes/`.
