# Weekly review

Trigger: scheduled (Sunday) or "run my weekly review."

1. Read root `MEMORY.md`, every workstation `MEMORY.md`, and `01_Me/Goals.md`.
2. Pull the coming week from the connected calendar.
3. Produce one page:
   - What moved last week (per workstation, 1 line each)
   - Open items older than 2 weeks (flag for decision or archive)
   - People follow-ups due (from `30_People/`)
   - Health: adherence vs. plan, in one line
   - AI learning: one line from the learning log
   - Proposed top 3 for the week
4. Ask the owner to confirm the top 3, then write them into root `MEMORY.md`.
