# 1:1 / partner meeting prep

Trigger: a calendar event with someone who has a file in `30_People/`, or "prep me for my meeting with <name>."

1. Read their `30_People/` file, and the `MEMORY.md` of each company they're linked to.
2. Search connected email/chat for the last two weeks of threads with them.
3. Produce a half-page: open commitments (mine and theirs), what's changed since last time, two questions worth asking, anything sensitive to handle carefully.
4. After the meeting, if the owner pastes notes: update their person file (last 1:1 entry, open items).
