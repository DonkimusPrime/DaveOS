# Brief

Trigger: "brief me on <topic/person/company/file>."

1. Locate everything relevant: the matching workstation, person or company files, recent connected email/calendar items, and any files named in the request.
2. Produce a one-page brief: what it is, current state, open decisions, who's involved, what's next, anything time-sensitive. Facts with sources (file path or thread), no padding.
3. End with the two questions the owner most likely needs answered.
4. Do not update any MEMORY.md — a brief is read-only.
