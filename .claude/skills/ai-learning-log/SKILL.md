# AI learning log (weekly)

Trigger: scheduled (Friday) or "AI learning check-in."

1. Read `10_Personal/AI-Learning/MEMORY.md` and `Notes/learning-log.md`.
2. Ask: what did AI do this week that saved time or produced something you couldn't have easily? What failed or felt clumsy?
3. Log both. If a failure points at a missing skill, connector, or template, propose it.
4. Suggest one concrete thing to try next week, tied to the 90-day target.
