# Build the people roster

Trigger: onboarding step 8, or "add people."

1. Ask the owner to list people by company: name, role, relationship to them.
2. Create one file per person in `30_People/` from `_TEMPLATE-Person.md`. Fill only what was stated.
3. Add each person to the "Who's here" section of the relevant company `CLAUDE.md` as a link.
4. Ask which relationships they most want to manage proactively, and mark those with a `## Priority` line.
